---
name: Supervisor evidence consent
description: Why private evidence review uses explicit course-scoped assignments rather than content ownership or profile roles.
---

For the assignment-based review flow, require an explicit
participant–supervisor assignment within the course; do not infer a workplace
supervisory relationship from course authorship or user-editable role metadata.
Keep this separate from course-owner coaching, where the matching course owner
is intentionally allowed to sign evidence linked to participant progress, but
not arbitrary files in the same folder.

**Why:** A course creator may publish material for many unrelated workplaces.
Being the content author is not evidence of a supervisory relationship, so
granting author-wide proof access would cross participant privacy boundaries.

**How to apply:** Keep this distinction when adding review inboxes, organization
roles, notifications, exports, and signed evidence links. Do not silently remove
course-owner coaching access when applying assignment-based rules. Revocation
blocks new access but cannot retract already issued signed links until their
short expiry.
