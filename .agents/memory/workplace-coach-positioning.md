---
name: Workplace Coach positioning
description: Product boundary and differentiation for Chaesa Live microlearning.
---

Chaesa Live microlearning is a cross-industry AI Workplace Coach. Construction remains the first and strongest domain pack, but the core product must work for any role or industry.

**Why:** The product must not become a smaller duplicate of the existing LMS, BIMTEK catalog, or construction AI assistant. Its distinctive outcome is a short workplace mission that turns real context into practice, evidence, feedback, and repetition.

**How to apply:** Frame new microlearning features around the loop “one skill, one practice, one proof.” Keep formal courses and certification in the LMS, training discovery in BIMTEK, and open-ended expert problem solving in KonstruksiAI.

Use a two-dimensional operating model when explaining or extending the product:

- The vertical dimension connects organizational priorities, role context, one behavioral mission, practice with evidence, and team-level learning.
- The horizontal dimension runs from a workplace trigger through context, mission, practice, evidence, feedback, and scheduled repetition.
- Sector packs adapt terminology, risks, standards, scenarios, and acceptable evidence. They do not change the core practice loop.

**Why:** This keeps Chaesa Live useful across sectors without becoming generic. Every sector can preserve its own operational language while sharing one repeatable execution system.

**How to apply:** New domain packs, dashboards, evidence flows, supervisor validation, and analytics should map to both dimensions: where they sit vertically and which step of the horizontal loop they improve.
