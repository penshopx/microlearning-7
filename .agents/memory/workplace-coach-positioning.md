---
name: Workplace Coach positioning
description: Product boundary and differentiation for Chaesa Live microlearning.
---

Chaesa Live microlearning is a cross-industry AI Workplace Coach. Construction remains the first and strongest domain pack, but the core product must work for any role or industry.

**Why:** The product must not become a smaller duplicate of the existing LMS, BIMTEK catalog, or construction AI assistant. Its distinctive outcome is a short workplace mission that turns real context into practice, evidence, feedback, and repetition.

**How to apply:** Frame new microlearning features around the loop “one skill, one practice, one proof.” Keep formal courses and certification in the LMS, training discovery in BIMTEK, and open-ended expert problem solving in KonstruksiAI.
